How It Works

Step 01 — Onboarding

Employee added by HR in seconds

HR creates a new employee profile — department, designation, salary structure, role and documents are all set at once. The employee receives login credentials and gets immediate access to their self-service portal.

Photo upload and ID document storage
Auto-generated employee ID
Role assigned on creation — instant access
Probation period tracking begins automatically

Step 02 — Payroll

Automated payroll with one click

HR runs monthly payroll — the system automatically applies each employee's gross, tax, deductions and allowances. Payslips are generated as formatted PDFs and can be bulk-exported in Excel for finance.

Gross, tax, mobile, lunch, other deductions
Individual or bulk payslip PDF
Monthly Excel export for accounts
Employee self-view via portal
Step 03 — Leave & Attendance

From application to approval — fully digital

Employee applies for leave from the portal — manager receives a notification and approves/rejects. Leave balance updates instantly. Attendance is marked daily and late entries are flagged automatically.

Annual, casual, sick and special leave types
Manager approval with notification
Real-time balance deduction
Late arrival auto-flagging with alert
